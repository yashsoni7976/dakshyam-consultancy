# Refund Policy
Our commitment to transparency in financial transactions.
## 1. Service Fees
Consultancy fees paid for initial analysis, project report preparation, and documentation are generally non-refundable once the work has commenced. This is because these fees cover the professional time and expertise invested by our team.
## 2. Eligibility for Refund
Refunds may be considered only in the following specific circumstances:
- Duplicate payment made by mistake.
- Service cancellation requested within 24 hours of payment, provided no professional work has started.
- Failure on our part to deliver the committed documentation within the mutually agreed timeline (excluding delays caused by government portals or external factors).
## 3. Non-Refundable Situations
Fees will not be refunded if:
- The subsidy application is rejected by the government department due to eligibility issues or incorrect information provided by the client.
- The client decides to discontinue the business or project after the consultancy process has started.
- The client fails to provide the required documents within the stipulated time.
