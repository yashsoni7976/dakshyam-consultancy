Central Government Scheme
## AIF (Agriculture Infrastructure Fund)
Central Government scheme for new and existing Farmers, Agri-entrepreneurs, Startups, and Tech Agri-businesss.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/FINALSchemeGuidelinesAIF.pdf] AIF Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Interest subvention of 3% per annum up to a loan limit of ₹ 2 crore.
-
Subvention available upto 7 years.
-
100% reimbursement of CGTMSE fees, requiring no collateral.
-
Maximum 9% Rate of interest on Loans.
### Eligibility
-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership, Company, FPO & SHG.
### Eligible Projects
-
Post-Harvest Management Projects like warehouse, Agro processing, Poly house and viable farming assets.
State Government Scheme
## BRUPY (Bhimrao Ambedkar Rajasthan Dalit Adivasi Udhyam Protsahan Yojana)
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote entrepreneurship among Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) entrepreneurs.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/BRUPY_Scheme.pdf] BRUPY Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Capital Subsidy of 25% of Project cost up ₹ 25 Lakhs.
-
Interest subvention of 6% to 9% per annum up to a loan of ₹ 10 crore.
-
Subvention available upto 5 years.
### Eligibility
-
For Rajasthan Domicile SC / ST Entrepreneur.
-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership, Company where 51% or more ownership by SC/ST Individuals.
### Eligible Projects
-
All sectors of Manufacturing, Service and Traders.
State Government Scheme
## Mukhyamantri Nari Shakti Udyam Protsahan Yojana (MNSUPY)
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote entrepreneurship among Women Led entrepreneurs.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/Mahila_Schemes.pdf] MNSUPY Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Capital Subsidy of 25% of Project cost (max. ₹ 25 Lakhs).
-
Total Project Loan up to ₹ 1 crore.
-
Loan upto 10 Lakhs to Traders (Subsidy upto 2.50 Lakhs).
### Eligibility
-
For Rajasthan Domicile Women Entrepreneur.
-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership and Company where 100% ownership by Women.
### Eligible Projects
-
All sectors of Manufacturing and Service.
State Government Scheme
## MSME Policy 2024
Rajasthan government assistance scheme for new and existing MSME’s for digitalization and quality improvement.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/rajasthan-msme-policy-2024.pdf] Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
50% reimbursement on cost of quality certifications and Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) (Max. Rs 3 Lakhs).
-
Financial assistance in Participation in National / International Fairs / Exhibitions.
-
75% reimbursement on cost of new software or equipment for digitization of business process (Max. Rs 50,000).
-
75% reimbursement on total fees charged by e-commerce platforms (Max. Rs 50,000).
### Eligibility
-
New and existing MSMEs in Rajasthan.
### Eligible Projects
-
Digitalization, Quality Certification, IPR, E-commerce participation.
State Government Scheme
## MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana)
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote entrepreneurship among Young entrepreneurs.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/MYSY_Guidelines_Rajasthan.pdf] MYSY Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Interest subvention of 100% of interest charged by bank (for loans up to 10 lakhs).
-
10 Percent Capital Subsidy.
-
Reimbursement of 100% CGTMSE Fees.
-
Interest subvention for 5 Years.
### Eligibility
-
Age between 18 – 45 Years.
-
Educational Qualification minimum 8th Class.
-
For Rajasthan Domicile Entrepreneur.
-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership and Company where 51% or more ownership by Young entrepreneurs.
### Eligible Projects
-
For Trading, Manufacturing and Service sector.
Central Government Scheme
## PMEGP (Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme)
Central government subsidy scheme to promote entrepreneurship among new entrepreneurs.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/PMEGP_SCHEME.pdf] PMEGP Scheme.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Capital Subsidy of 15% to 35% of project cost.
-
Maximum subsidy upto Rs. 17.50 Lakhs.
### Eligibility
-
Minimum 8th Pass.
-
Individual / Proprietorship enterprises.
-
Own contribution of 5% to 10% of project cost.
### Eligible Projects
-
Maximum Project Cost Rs.50 lakhs for manufacturing Sector.
-
Maximum Project Cost Rs.20 Lakhs for Service Sector.
Central Government Scheme
## PMFME (PM Formalisation of Micro food processing Enterprises)
Central government subsidy scheme to promote Agro and Food Processing for new and existing micro enterprises.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/PMFME_GUIDELINES.pdf] PMFME Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Capital Subsidy 35% of project cost (Machine & Building Cost).
-
Maximum subsidy upto Rs. 10.00 Lakhs.
### Eligibility
-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership and Company.
-
Own contribution of 10% to 40% of Project Cost.
### Eligible Projects
-
Agro and Food manufacturing, processing, or packaging.
-
Maximum Project Cost Rs.20 Lakhs for Service Sector.
State Government Scheme
## ODOP (One District One Product) Rajasthan
A Rajasthan state initiative to identify, promote, and brand unique products from each district to boost local entrepreneurship and global exports.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/ODOP_Policy_Guidelines.pdf] ODOP Policy Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Special financial assistance for project setups related to district-specific products.
-
Marketing and branding support at National and International level.
-
Technical training and skill development for district clusters.
-
Preferential treatment in government procurement for ODOP units.
### Eligibility
-
New and Existing units manufacturing or processing the identified district product.
-
Rajasthan Domicile entrepreneurs, MSMEs, and Co-operative clusters.
### Eligible Projects
-
District specific products like Blue Pottery (Jaipur), Woodwork (Jodhpur), Bikaneri Bhujia (Bikaner), Kota Stone (Kota), etc.
State Government Scheme
## Raj Trade Policy
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote retail, wholesale, and e-commerce in Rajasthan state.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/Govt-of-Rajasthan-issued-the-Rajasthan-Trade-Promotion-Policy-2025-29-JAN302026.pdf] Rajasthan Trade Promotion Policy 2025.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
Interest subvention of 6% for loans upto Rs. 1 Crore.
-
Interest subvention of 4% for loans between Rs. 1 Crore to Rs. 2 crore.
-
Reimbursement of 50% CGTMSE Fees.
-
Reimbursement of 50% Insurance Charges.
-
Interest subvention for 5 Years.
### Eligibility
-
New Retail and Wholesale Trading.
-
For Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership and Company.
### Eligible Projects
-
Working Capital Loan upto 80% of total loan.
State Government Scheme
## RIPS 2024 (Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme)
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote Manufacturing and Service sector for new and existing enterprises.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/RIPS_2024.pdf] RIPS 2024 Guidelines.pdf
### Key Benefits
-
50% Capital Subsidy upto Rs. 1.50 Crore for Agro and Food processing.
-
3% to 6% interest subvention for Loans upto 10 Crore for 7 Years.
-
100% Stamp Duty benefit for Land purchase or Lease.
-
100% Conversion charges benefit for Land conversion.
-
100% Exemption on Electricity Duty Charges for 7 Years.
-
100% Mandi Tax/fee Reimbursement for 7 Years.
-
75% of SGST subsidy for 10 Years.
### Eligibility
-
Manufacturing and Service sector (new and existing).
### Eligible Projects
-
Warehouse, Solar, Hotel, Resort Schemes also available.

## VYUPY (Vishwakarma Yuva Protsahan Yojana)
Rajasthan government subsidy scheme to promote entrepreneurship among Young entrepreneurs.
Check Eligibility & Consult
Documents
- [LINK /schemes/VYUPY_Guidelines.pdf] VYUPY Guidelines.pdf

-
Interest subvention of 7% to 8% of interest charged by bank.

25% Capital Subsidy (Max. Rs 5 Lakhs).

Interest subvention for 5 Years.

-
Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership and Company where 51% or more ownership by Young entrepreneurs.
### Eligible Projects
-
For New and Existing Manufacturing and Service sector.
