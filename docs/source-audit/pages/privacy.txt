# Privacy Policy
Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we handle your data.
## 1. Information We Collect
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, we collect only the personal data necessary for providing our services. This includes:
- Identity Data: Name, Business Name, Udyam Registration number.
- Contact Data: Email address, Phone number, Business address.
- Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information for security monitoring (ISMS compliance).
- Subsidy Documents: Financial statements and project reports provided voluntarily for analysis.
## 2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing
We process your data based on your explicit consent . The purposes include:
- Facilitating government subsidy applications (PMEGP, PMFME, etc.).
- Business consultancy and feasibility analysis.
- Sending requested updates and service notifications.
- Ensuring network and information security (ISO 27001).
## 3. Data Security & ISMS
We maintain an Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with ISO 27001 standards.
- Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest.
- Access Control: Strict role-based access to client documents.
- Monitoring: Continuous security logging and threat detection.
## 4. Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023
As a Data Principal, you have the following rights:
- Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data being processed.
- Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updates or deletion of your data.
- Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent at any time.
- Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at [LINK mailto:dpo@buzzpenny.in] dpo@buzzpenny.in .
